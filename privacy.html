Nuru privacy policy

Nuru is used by its owner only. It accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, read-only, and Gmail drafts) to build a private knowledge base on the owner's own computer.

Data obtained through Google APIs is stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's own encrypted backup. Parts of it are sent to an AI model provider only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, not shared for any other purpose, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalised AI or machine learning models.

Nuru's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke Nuru's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
